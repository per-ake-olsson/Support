# Privacy Policy for Key2Voice Windows

This supplementary privacy information describes **how Key2Voice specifically handles audio, text, and locally stored information**. Eureka Consulting AB's general personal data policy describes, among other things, how personal data is processed when you contact support, your rights, and how to contact us about privacy matters.

**1. Speech Recognition and Cloud Processing**  
When you use speech recognition or translation, Key2Voice sends audio and the information needed for processing to **Microsoft Azure Speech**, using the Azure Speech resource that you have configured yourself. Speech recognition is therefore not an entirely local function. Information about Microsoft's processing, storage, and terms is available in Microsoft's Azure Speech documentation and the terms of your Azure subscription.

**2. Audio Sources and Transcripts**  
You choose when Key2Voice processes speech from a microphone, system audio, or a WAV file. The transcribed text may contain personal data or other sensitive information, depending on what is said or played back. You are responsible for using the feature with due regard for the privacy of the people concerned and applicable rules.

**3. Local Storage and Protection**  
Key2Voice uses a locally encrypted vault for configuration and saved information, including transcripts that you choose to save. Protect the vault password and make backups if the information is important. Eureka Consulting AB cannot recover a lost private vault passphrase.

**4. Synchronization and Backups**  
With Premium, you can create an encrypted sync file to transfer settings to iPhone or export selected transcripts. Azure Speech credentials are included in a normal sync export only if you explicitly choose to include them; they are not included in selective transcript exports. Portable export and import can be used for backup and restore even in Basic. You are responsible for managing the files, their passwords, and how they are transferred or stored. The sync fingerprint is used to verify the sender's identity during sync import.

**5. Processing by Other Services**  
Windows, Microsoft Store, and Microsoft Azure may process information in accordance with their respective terms and privacy notices. For information about Microsoft's handling of audio and other data when using Azure Speech, see the documentation and terms for your own Azure subscription.
