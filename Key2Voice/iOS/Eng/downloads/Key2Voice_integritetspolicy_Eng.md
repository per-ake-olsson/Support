# Privacy Policy for Key2Voice iOS

This supplementary privacy information describes **Key2Voice-specific handling of audio, text, and locally stored information** on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. Eureka Consulting AB's general personal data policy describes, among other things, how personal data is processed when you contact support, your rights, and how to contact us about privacy matters.

**1. Speech Recognition and Cloud Processing**  
When you start speech recognition or translation, Key2Voice sends audio and the necessary information from your iPhone or iPad to **Microsoft Azure Speech**, using the Azure Speech resource you have configured. Speech recognition therefore does not take place entirely on the device. Microsoft's terms and privacy information for Azure Speech and your subscription apply to the cloud service's processing.

**2. Audio and Transcribed Text**  
You decide when your iPhone or iPad starts or stops listening for speech recognition. The text displayed or saved may contain personal data or sensitive information, depending on what is said. Use the feature with consideration for the privacy of those concerned and applicable rules.

**3. Local Storage and Protection**  
Key2Voice uses a locally encrypted vault on iPhone or iPad for configuration and app information, including saved transcripts. The information is protected by the app's vault management and iOS security features. Keep sync files and their passwords secure; Eureka Consulting AB cannot recover lost private passwords.

**4. Synchronization and Transfer**  
iPhone and iPad can import an encrypted sync file created in Key2Voice for Windows. The import checks the file's sender and integrity. The user transfers the sync file; this is not automatic cloud synchronization through Eureka Consulting AB. Azure Speech credentials are included in a normal sync export from Windows only when explicitly selected, and are not included in a selective transcript export. Protect the sync file and its password.

**5. Apple Watch and Other Services**  
If you use Apple Watch together with iPhone, the iPhone sends the current transcribed text and session status to the watch. The watch can send commands to start and stop speech recognition on the iPhone. Apple Watch does not perform its own Azure speech recognition and does not receive Azure keys or a separate vault containing transcript history through this feature. Apple and Microsoft may process information in accordance with their respective service terms and privacy notices.
