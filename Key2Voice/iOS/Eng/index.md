## **Key2Voice** – Speech to Text and Translation
Copyright 2026 Eureka Consulting AB

Key2Voice for iPhone or iPad helps you understand and follow speech by displaying it as readable text in real time. The app uses the iPhone or iPad microphone for speech recognition and can translate between supported languages. You can also save and manage transcripts. **Apple Watch can be used together with iPhone** as an optional companion to read the current text and start or stop speech recognition. The iPhone handles audio capture and speech processing even when you use Apple Watch.

**Key2Voice for iPhone and iPad is free** and does not require a separate Premium purchase. However, to use the app you must first configure Key2Voice for Windows and import an encrypted sync file containing the necessary information to your iPhone or iPad. **Sync Export from Windows requires Premium.** You also need your own working **Microsoft Azure Speech resource** and an internet connection. Azure is a separate service that may incur charges; Windows Premium does not include an Azure subscription or Azure usage.

This page provides usage and support information, documentation, contact details, and the privacy policy for Key2Voice for iPhone and iPad, with Apple Watch as an iPhone companion. Select one of the links below for more information.
